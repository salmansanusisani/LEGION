## Suggested 3-minute sequence

1. **0:00–0:20 — Context.** Introduce LEGION and Tablekeeper. Explain that reservations must remain correct under contention, retries and changes.
2. **0:20–0:55 — Real factory.** Show the official BAND room, four named seats, the initial task and a real addressed handoff. Show a real rejection/repair if one occurred; do not invent one. Hide account secrets, not substantive work history.
3. **0:55–1:45 — Delivered product.** Use the accepted stage's browser to search, sign in, book, look up and amend a reservation. Show a combined-table choice and clear feedback. If fewer stages are accepted, demonstrate only their actual scope.
4. **1:45–2:20 — Advanced correctness.** For an accepted stage 4, preview and apply a seating repair and demonstrate a recurring amendment. Show unchanged booking identity/terms and the resulting history, not just a success toast.
5. **2:20–2:45 — Evidence.** Show an exact accepted Git SHA, isolated official test results and independent UX review. Mention offline container operation and disclose any environment adapter or limitation.
6. **2:45–3:00 — Handoff.** Show the public repo, stage RUN instructions, FACTORY documentation and complete untouched room export.
